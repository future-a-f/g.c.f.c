Promote our events

Why this matters for GCFC

Many people meet the church first through the internet. This site should reflect our values, invite participation, and make spiritual connection simple.

  • Help people learn about our services, ministries, and prayer support before they visit.
  • Enable church members to stay connected with events, teaching, and community news.
  • Show the heart of our leadership, our mission, and our outreach work with confidence.

How the website will serve the church

Responsive and accessible

Designed for phones, tablets, and desktop screens so everyone can use it.

Easy updates

Content can be refreshed quickly for sermons, events, announcements, and prayer requests.

Clear structure

Dedicated pages for events, messages, worship details, and contact information.

The platform will support the church’s story, schedule, giving guidance, and prayer ministry in a single, trusted place.

What we will collect

Church identity

  • Mission, values, and regional focus
  • Leadership and ministry team introductions

Teaching

  • Sermon summaries and resources
  • Current teaching series and topics

Events

  • Conference dates and assembly schedules
  • Outreach programs and prayer gatherings

Media

  • Logo, photos, and church branding details

Support

  • Giving information and project updates
  • Prayer requests and pastoral contact details

Expected result

  • An inviting online presence that reflects GCFC’s heart and ministry.
  • A convenient resource for guests, members, and ministry partners.
  • A church website that supports worship, teaching, and service throughout the year.

Suggested launch timeline

Launch within 4–6 weeks with an initial version that highlights services, events, and prayer support. The site can evolve with additional teaching and media content over time.
