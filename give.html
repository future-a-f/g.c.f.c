Giving principles

  • Voluntary: Giving is a joyful response, not pressure.
  • Cheerful: God loves a cheerful giver — attitude matters.
  • Prayerful: Part of worship and dependence on God.
  • Faithful: Regular modest gifts often sustain ministry best.
  • Transparent: Funds handled with integrity before God and the church.
