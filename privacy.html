Privacy policy

How we collect, use, and protect information when you use this website or our contact form.

Last updated: May 14, 2026

Introduction

Grace Community Fellowship Church (“we,” “us,” “our,” or “Church”) is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website.

Information we collect

Information you provide

When you fill out our contact form or submit prayer requests, you may provide:

  • Your name, email address, and phone number
  • Prayer requests and personal information shared in messages
  • Other information you voluntarily provide

Information collected automatically

When you visit our website, we may automatically collect limited information such as:

  • Browser type and operating system
  • Pages you visit and time spent on each page
  • IP address (for security and analytics, if enabled)

How we use your information

  • To respond to prayer requests and inquiries
  • To provide ministry support and pastoral care
  • To improve our website and user experience
  • To send occasional ministry updates and event announcements (where you have agreed)
  • To fulfill legal obligations

Protection of your information

We use reasonable measures to protect personal information. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure; we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Sharing your information

We do not sell your personal information. Prayer requests and contact details are treated confidentially and shared only with pastoral staff who need the information to provide care.

Third-party links

This policy applies to our site only. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of external websites linked from our pages.
